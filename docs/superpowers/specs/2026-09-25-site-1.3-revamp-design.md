# fathom site revamp for 1.3: design

Status: approved plan, in progress. Branch `revamp/1.3.0` (from `privacy/1.3.0`).
This spec replaces the homepage sections of the 2026-05-30 and 2026-09-05 specs.
The 05-30 AAA acceptance criteria and YAGNI list still apply.

## Why

fathom 1.3 turns the app into one conversation. You say what you need, and it
looks, reads, guides, and remembers. The site still sells a five-mode indoor
navigation app. The revamp repositions it as visual assistance you can talk to
and rebuilds every phone screen from the 1.3 app.

## Sources of truth

- **App reference build:** project-homer `.worktrees/device-test` at `ba8e462`
  (1.3.0 build 17). Every app string on the site comes from it, recorded with its
  file and line in `lib/app-facts.ts`. `scripts/check-app-facts.mjs` re-checks
  them at a given SHA.
- **Design system:** the app's design system (artifact
  https://claude.ai/artifact/62m3pWCm3uSiyouXi5WkXA, mirrored in project-homer
  `docs/design-system/`). The site vendors `tokens.json` and the logo SVGs into
  `design-system/vendor/` and generates CSS from them. The site never edits a
  vendored token.
- **Live screens:** the iPhone 17 Simulator running the reference build. Its
  captures are kept outside this repo and used to check the mockups.

## Decisions

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Hero line | "Visual assistance you can talk to." |
| Brand casing | "fathom" is always lowercase, including "fathom plus". Exceptions: "Fathom: Visual Assistance" (the App Store name), Apple's own labels, "Unruly Vision, LLC". |
| Typeface | Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, with Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono for distances and counters (SIL Open Font License, free). The Braille Institute drew it with low-vision readers, the people fathom is for. Body text sits close to SF Pro in width and proportion; headlines are set at 500 to 600 with slightly tighter tracking, as SF Display does. Zed (Typotheque) was the first pick but is over budget; Mona Sans 2.0 is the runner-up if a closer SF look is wanted. |
| Numerals | Tabular figures for counters and distances ("2 of 5", "about 7 to 10 feet"). |
| Appearance | Dark (default) and light, through the existing toggle. `prefers-contrast: more` switches to the design system's Contrast Boost values on its own, with no control. |
| Phone screens | Drawn in HTML and CSS from the design system's tokens and component specs, not screenshots, so they follow the theme and animate on scroll. |
| Scroll machinery | Kept as built: sticky phone, pinned hero, cover plane, progress rail, step announcer, Pause motion. Only the screens and content change. |
| Legal pages | Restyled, never reworded. |
| Launch | Production the day 1.3 is on the App Store. The privacy update ships earlier, on its own. |

## Story

Hero → the gap → ten steps → safety net → day to day → download.

Each phone plays a short scene and rests on the frame in the last column,
which is also what reduced motion, Pause motion and no-JS readers see.

| # | Step | Phone (scene) | Resting frame | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Just ask | Conversation: idle, listening, thinking, speaking | The question and the answer in the transcript | Free. Talking uses Cloud AI |
| 2 | Look Now | More sheet rises: six Look Now rows, then Saved | The sheet, "Read text" picked | Free |
| 3 | Read | Look Now's "Document. 5 items.", then the readout on the stage | Speaking, the bar at "2 of 5", the transport | Free |
| 4 | Lookout and Point to Ask | Activity card talking; a finger holds the center to point | fathom speaking, Pause | Free |
| 5 | Memory | "Remember …" and fathom's echo, then Settings' Memory list | The new memory in the Memory list | Free |
| 6 | Go | Go card, "Getting close", "Almost there", "It looks like…" | The arrival question, with its answers | fathom plus |
| 7 | Step-by-step plans | You ask; "Your plan" rises and its steps arrive | "Your plan", Accept plan / Edit / Dismiss | fathom plus |
| 8 | Task and Live mode | Live card, "Microphone off", listening, sending | The reply over the card, "Microphone off" | fathom plus |
| 9 | Skills | The offer spoken; you say "save this as a skill" | fathom's "Saved…" and the new skill atop the suggestions | fathom plus |
| 10 | Your choice | "Allow cloud AI?" rises and reads to its end | The consent sheet at its top, both answers | Free |

The illustrative examples follow one thread (laundry), so memory, Go, the plan,
Task and skills connect.

## Truth rules

- No claim that fathom sees, verifies or guarantees something it can't. Go asks
  whether you've arrived. "Always on" is only for obstacle alerts. Never "nothing
  leaves your phone". No hazard-sound claims (off in the reference build).
- Every string inside a phone is an app fact or a marked example.
  `test/helpers/copy-rules.ts` enforces this, along with the retired names, no em
  dashes, American English, and lowercase fathom, on every marketing surface.

## Architecture

- `design-system/vendor/`: vendored `tokens.json`, logo SVGs, app icon.
  `SOURCE.json` pins the source SHA and hashes.
- `scripts/ds/sync.mjs` copies from the app checkout and refuses a dirty source.
  `scripts/ds/build.mjs` generates:
  - `generated/fathom-tokens.css` (`--fathom-*`, dark, light, and Contrast Boost
    under `prefers-contrast: more`; type scale in rem; motion);
  - `generated/site-roles.css` (`--site-*`, the AAA layer: every value is a
    design-system variable);
  - `generated/tokens.ts` (resolved hex for OG images, theme color and tests);
  - `generated/logo-paths.ts` (logo outlines with their paths unchanged).
- `app/globals.css` maps the tokens into Tailwind v4 with `@theme inline`. The
  accessibility blocks stay as they are.
- `components/brand/` (Mark with the size ladder, Lockup, Logotype) and
  `components/phone/` (primitives, the scene engine, and one folder per screen).
- `lib/app-facts.ts` (app strings) and `lib/landing-content.ts` (`COPY`, the site's
  own words).

## Site-only rules (to write back to the design system)

- The AAA role layer: light secondary text on fog-800; light accent text on
  fathom-900; the CTA is fathom-900 with bone text; no pure white on the web
  (bone-0 renders as bone-25); tertiary text is never page copy.
- Marketing display sizes above `display-lg`.
- Web typeface: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next and Mono, as above (the app stays on SF Pro).
- Glyphs in mockups are drawn stand-ins; SF Symbols can't be redistributed.
- Page type and display, in `app/site-tokens.css`: `--site-type-optical-scale`
  1.06 (Atkinson's cap height against SF Pro's), `--site-type-display-weight` 500,
  `--site-type-hero-tracking` -0.02em and `--site-type-section-tracking` -0.015em,
  and the rem-based display sizes (hero, section, feature, download, and the
  feature size on phones).
- Phone mockups (`components/phone/phone.css`, the `.phone-screen` block):
  metrics components.md gives without a token (status bar 50px, island 76 by
  23, usage button 42, sheet row 60, readout position 80, glow reach 220, glow
  strength 16% and 7.2%, glow floor 0.9, orb halo 30%, the orb's boosted ring
  2.34 in its own units, the orb's periods), `--ph-glyph-scale` 1.35,
  `--ph-press-scale` 0.97, `--ph-weight-medium` 500 and `--ph-weight-semibold`
  600, and `--mr-segment-inset` 3px (FathomSegmentedControl).
- The scene engine and its conventions (`components/phone/README.md`): a scene
  is a list of beats that rests on its last; taps hold `TAP_MS` (600ms) with a
  finger ring (`--ph-touch-fill`, `--ph-touch-edge`); `--ph-word-pace`,
  `--ph-stagger`, `--ph-stagger-lead`; turns arrive whole; the suggestion strip
  folds on the settle curve. None of these is app UI: they pace a mockup.
- Weakest app roles inside the mockups, for the design system to know: light
  text-secondary is 5.16:1 on bg-default and text-tertiary 4.64:1 (AA, not AAA).
  The mockups keep the app's colors; they are decorative and the step copy
  carries the meaning.
- Open for the design system: FathomButton's corner radius is 12 on its card but
  16 in the README and tokens.
